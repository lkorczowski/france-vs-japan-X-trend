§06· Method

How this is measured

  1. Data comes from the official X API: post counts per day, and the public metrics of tracked posts.
  2. Queries.
  3. Volume counts posts in the last full UTC day. Heat divides volume by the daily average from 15 Sep to 15 Oct 2026. Firepower adds the likes and reposts that each side's 15 most viewed posts gained since the previous update. Hot posts are found through the posts people quote the most.
  4. Sides are the language of the post, not the nationality of the author.
  5. Post text is never displayed, by choice. Posts that match a slur blocklist are never tracked.
